\section*{Research Highlights}
\textit{Closed-Loop Proportional--Integral Feedback and Linear-Time Bipartite Spectral Control for Fault-Tolerant Multi-Modal Clustering}

\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{6pt}
    \item Closed-loop PI and dual eigengap control regulates multi-modal spectral clustering.
    \item Damped PI mirror descent gives ISS and Davis-Kahan bounds without dead-zone stall.
    \item Bipartite Gram decomposition achieves exact O(nm\^{}2) equivalence on 90k-300k cells.
    \item Smooth and Null-SNR PI cut severe-fault leakage to 0.012 and 0.002 (q=1.46e-11).
    \item 536-point gain phase map and 200k-cell 7-step recovery verify stability margins.
\end{itemize}
